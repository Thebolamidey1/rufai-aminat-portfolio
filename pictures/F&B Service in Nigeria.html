These early chains marked a turning point in Nigeria’s urban dining culture, introducing standardization, franchising, and a more modern eating-out experience to Nigerians. Over time, this early wave of eateries laid the groundwork for a more competitive and dynamic market.

Growth and Expansion

Fast forward to the 2000s and 2010s, the Nigerian F&B landscape expanded significantly with the entrance of new brands like Chicken Republic and Domino’s Pizza, offering globalized menus and more contemporary restaurant designs. These new entrants introduced tech-enabled services such as delivery apps, digital menus, and online payments—enhancing customer convenience.

In addition, a new generation of private upscale restaurants, especially on Lagos Island, emerged catering to a niche clientele.   • Urbanization Boost: The establishment of eateries contributes to real estate development and infrastructure growth.

  • Cultural Exchange: Exposure to international brands and food types has diversified Nigerian palates while promoting local fusion cuisines.

Negative Impacts

Conclusion

The food and beverage service sector in Nigeria has journeyed from the iconic Mr Biggs era to a competitive and diverse ecosystem of local and global brands.
